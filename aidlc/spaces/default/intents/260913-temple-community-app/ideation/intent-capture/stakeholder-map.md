# Stakeholder Map — Digamber Jain Temple Community App

## Key Stakeholders and Their Interests

| Stakeholder | Interest | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Project owner and maintainer (the community member building the app) | Builds and maintains the app for the temple, and carries the work itself | [Q5] |
| Temple trustees | Sign off informally as needed, and are consulted on major changes such as what counts as an admin and the donation account details | [Q5] [Q6] |

## Audience Served

These groups use the app but were not named as accountable stakeholders.

| Group | Relationship to the app | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The broader public beyond the existing temple community, including visitors and prospective donors who are not members | The audience the app is built for | [Q2] |
| Signed-in users | Reach administrative functions, donating, and submitting feedback, all of which require sign-in | [Q4a] |
| Anyone, signed in or not | Reach the content feed and the PDF library, which are public information and require no sign-in | [Q4a] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

| Party | Authority | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Project owner and maintainer | Decision-maker — decides scope and priority going forward | [Q6] |
| Temple trustees | Influencers — consulted informally on major changes such as the definition of an admin and donation account details, and sign off informally as needed | [Q5] [Q6] |

## Communication Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting cadence | Informal — there is no set cadence; the trustees are shown the app when it is ready to use | [Q7] |
